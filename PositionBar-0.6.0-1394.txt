PositionBar 0.6.0

Improved
- Upgrading clears previous local portfolio history. Supported accounts sync again using their saved settings and credentials, and history starts accumulating from that sync.
- Kraken account support has ended. Kraken accounts are excluded from the portfolio, and public USDT/USD and USDC/USD quotes now come from Bitstamp.
- Charts stay visible during refresh, and expanding an account scrolls its holdings into view.
- Asset details keep the last valid values during refresh. Select an underlined asset name to open its public market page when available.
- U.S. stock valuations use the latest verified quote available from Longbridge and Tiger, including pre-market, after-hours and overnight sessions.

New
- Statistics now stacks equity history by account. Hover over the chart to see the account breakdown at that time.
- Binance Global now supports Portfolio Margin, Portfolio Margin Pro and SPAN. Sync also covers Funding, margin, Simple Earn, staking and Crypto Loan where available.
- Turn the local MCP service on or off in Integrations settings.

Fixed
- Daily P&L uses each account’s first complete snapshot of the day when it has no midnight baseline.
- Fixed pinned panels jumping between displays and filled gaps in translations for account setup and provider errors.
