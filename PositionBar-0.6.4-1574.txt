PositionBar 0.6.4

Improved
- Portfolio, positions and equity history now share one rule: use the latest known price and exchange rate at or before each valuation time. If a quote or rate has not refreshed, keep its last known value and timestamp.
- Equity chart amount ticks now line up with the value column on the right, bands for smaller accounts remain visible, and hover shows only the date, equity and account amounts.
- Price charts keep only the vertical guide; trades remain marked with their own symbols.

Fixed
- Fixed equity curves breaking and shifting during refresh.
- Fixed the offset inner dot on trade markers so markers and the hover guide line up.
