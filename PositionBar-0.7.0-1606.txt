PositionBar 0.7.0

New
- Added Tonghuashun Investment Ledger support with in-app web sign-in for ordinary CNY Shanghai and Shenzhen A-share ledgers without margin liability, including CNY cash, total equity, stock and ETF holdings, and holding P&L.

Improved
- Unified daily P&L and return calculations: the portfolio and accounts use net equity change, while individual positions and cash rows segment intraday trades and margin adjustments to measure daily P&L and return on cumulative effective capital.
- Contract rows in the position list now calculate position value from invested margin plus unrealized P&L across isolated, cross and portfolio margin modes, while detail popovers continue to show signed notional exposure.
- Redesigned chart hover readouts with a translucent panel-tinted surface that automatically docks to the upper-left or upper-right corner, delayed background chart loading indicators, and softened equity chart account colors.
- Polished macOS panel interactions: account headers use flexible widths with a hover menu, selected holdings stay highlighted while details are open, scrolling the list closes open details, and the top P&L switcher, toolbar icons and Settings account sidebar have cleaner proportions.
- Updated the macOS app icon with a tighter edge-fused tray and refined chip proportions, and updated the About window with crisp Light and Dark app icons.

Fixed
- Daily P&L now loads from local cache on the first frame at launch, and background refreshes or live quotes no longer clear displayed daily P&L or flash a syncing status.
- Fixed missing list values for Binance Portfolio Margin and OKX cross-margin contracts, and allowed negative values when cross-margin losses exceed invested margin.
- Fixed expand and collapse lag when toggling long account groups.
