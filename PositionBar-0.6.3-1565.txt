PositionBar 0.6.3

Improved
- Positions, accounts and the portfolio use consistent rules for daily and holding P&L. Remaining position costs use exchange rates from the relevant dates when available.
- Account rows show the amount with the selected P&L in parentheses. Asset details show P&L amounts and returns on separate rows.
- Right-click an account heading to change how its positions are sorted.

Fixed
- At launch, cached positions appear while daily P&L and holding costs update in the background.
- Clicking a position opens its details immediately. The details stay open if its row briefly disappears during a refresh.
