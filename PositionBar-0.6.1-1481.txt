PositionBar 0.6.1

New
- Price charts for supported assets can now mark buy and sell executions.
- iCloud Sync is on by default. It uses end-to-end encrypted iCloud Keychain to sync accounts, credentials, and shared display preferences across your Apple devices. You can turn it off in General settings.
- Refresh an individual account with Sync Now in its menu. You can add the same platform account as separate, independently managed connections.

Improved
- Scrolling and switching between Positions and Statistics are smoother. Equity history stays visible while data refreshes.
- Very small numbers use subscript zeros. Quantities in the portfolio list use up to four significant digits.
- The display currency is now limited to supported fiat currencies. An existing USDT display preference switches to USD.
- Automatic update checks run daily by default and respect your saved update preferences.
- Longbridge Hong Kong warrants and CBBCs use their own HKD prices. Improved Tiger option details and valuation of supported Coinbase US futures.

Fixed
- Fixed daily P&L baselines for individual holdings, holding P&L for OKX contracts, and valuation and returns for Binance options.
- Combined USDT wallet balances into one row per Binance account.
- Fixed an issue that could prevent asset details from opening when clicking a holding on macOS.
- Fixed quitting PositionBar while the privacy statement is open.
